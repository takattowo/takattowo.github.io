What am I doing at this point

2026-10-18

Been at a big company for years and the thing that gets me most isnt even the workload anymore.

We get Responsible AI trainings, Responsible AI checklists, Responsible AI everything. Responsible for what? AI writes the spec, AI writes the code, AI writes the tests, AI reviews it, AI writes the postmortem when it breaks. Where exactly is the responsible part?

Someone will say "AI works for us, we dont work for it, every change has an owner who has to explain it". Ok sure. Go ask that owner to explain it, live, without opening Claude. Most cant. Not bc theyre bad engineers but bc they got like 5 min to approve 3000 lines so the sprint looks good. Thats not ownership, thats just signing your name so theres someone to blame later. And if AI really works for us why are ppl doing 13 hr days just to keep up with it?

And pride is just gone. You used to build something smart and ppl noticed, you felt good about it. Now you build something actually clever and everyone just assumes Claude did it. Even when you designed the whole thing yourself. Nobody says nice work anymore bc whats the point, the AI did it right?

So idk. We're accountable for stuff we never had time to understand, and we get no credit for the stuff we actually did understand. How is that responsible?
