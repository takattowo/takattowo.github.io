What am I doing at this point

2026-10-18 2 revisions

I've always been a learning nerd. Certs, new tech, side projects just to see if I could pull them off.

Back then, finishing something felt great. Passing an exam after weeks of studying felt like a big deal. I could actually see myself getting better.

Now I'm building way cooler stuff than I ever have. I know more, the tools are better, and I can make things I couldn't have pictured a few years ago.

And yet... I don't really get that buzz anymore.

I think part of it is how easy everything is now. Stuff that used to take me weeks of trial and error, I can throw together in an afternoon with AI. The result's probably better. It just doesn't feel earned the same way.

I'm still learning and still building. But some days I can't tell if I'm actually getting better, or just getting better at telling AI what to do.

Kinda weird. More skills, more certs, and better projects than ever, and somehow I feel less proud than I did as a beginner.

I miss finishing something and just being proud of it, without that little voice asking how much of it was really me.
