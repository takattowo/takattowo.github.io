ResuMeme

A small project for learning the Azure stack end-to-end: Static Web Apps, managed Functions, Blob Storage, and Azure OpenAI. The app takes an uploaded CV (PDF or DOCX), parses it, runs the content through an LLM, and renders the result back to the user. It deploys via azd to a Free-tier SWA + Standard LRS storage account, and authenticates to Azure OpenAI with a key.

This premium giga-VIP app turns your CV/resume into an absolute job-hunting war machine, sigma alpha gigachad style. Guaranteed your applications won't get ignored anymore. HR and recruiters will finish reading your CV and start calling you at 2 AM begging to schedule an interview.

Powered by a self-hosted GPT 5.5 model to sigmafy your CV, completely free (from my own wallet 🗿). Once I run out of money, the app shuts itself down.
