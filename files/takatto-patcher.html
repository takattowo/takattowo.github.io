FS2 Maiden

FS2 Maiden (former name takattoFS2 Patcher) is a community made patcher for Freestyle2: Street Basketball that enhances the game with quality of life tweaks, mods, translations, and downloadable custom content. It simplifies applying patches to the game client while supporting multiple servers, including Global, Korea, and China, helping keep the game fresh and enjoyable for players.

Hand crafted with over 40 thousand lines of code, no AI assistance involved. Built back when Stack Overflow was the real co-pilot.

This project was where I first learned reverse engineering, memory patching, file modding, and a lot of low level game internals through pure experimentation and curiosity. And indeed, I got lot of my accounts banned LOL.
